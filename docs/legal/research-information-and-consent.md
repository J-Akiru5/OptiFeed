## 1. Invitation

You are invited to take part in a capstone research study that evaluates the PakaonAI Precision Feeding System. The study will be conducted with **20 fish farmers** and **10 IT professionals**. Your participation is voluntary. Please read this form, ask questions, and decide whether you want to take part.

## 2. Purpose

The study develops and evaluates a precision feeding system for African catfish. It combines an ESP32-based feeder with a web dashboard that recalculates feed amounts from manual biomass samples. The evaluation uses the **ISO/IEC 25010:2023** software quality model to assess functionality, reliability, compatibility, security, usability, maintainability, efficiency, and portability.

## 3. What you will be asked to do

If you agree, you will complete a questionnaire about the PakaonAI web dashboard and feeder hardware. You will rate statements about the system and may add comments. The questionnaire is available on **printed paper** or through **Google Forms**.

- You may skip any question.
- You may stop at any time.
- You do not need to provide your name.
- We will not audio-record, video-record, or photograph you.
- We will not ask for your email address, phone number, or other contact details in the form.

You may answer at your own pace.

## 4. Information we collect

| Item | Required? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Optional | You may leave it blank. |
| Date | Yes | Used to organize the responses. |
| Role | Yes | IT expert, hardware/electronics expert, agriculturist, or farm operator/field expert. |
| Questionnaire answers | Yes | Your ratings and optional comments about the system. |

We do **not** collect email addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, health information, biometrics, precise location, audio, video, or photos. Please do not write sensitive personal information or another person's data on the form.

## 5. Confidentiality

- Only the four researchers and the thesis adviser will have access to the raw responses.
- Printed forms are kept in a **locked cabinet** accessible only to the researchers.
- Google Forms responses stay in Google Forms/Drive and are accessible only to the researchers. The form is configured **not to collect email addresses**.
- Results are reported in **aggregated or anonymized** form.
- We will not name you, quote you, or publish your photo without separate written permission.
- The capstone paper may be published in a reputable research journal. Only aggregated or anonymized results will be included.

## 6. Risks and benefits

There are minimal risks. You may feel uncomfortable answering some questions; you can skip them or stop. There is no direct payment or guaranteed benefit. Your participation helps evaluate a precision feeding system for smallholder catfish farms and contributes to the capstone research.

## 7. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may refuse, skip questions, or withdraw at any time without penalty. To withdraw, contact the Data Protection Officer at **privacy@pakaonai.syntaxure.dev**. Withdrawal stops future processing. Data already aggregated or published in a form that no longer identifies you may not be removable.

## 8. Retention

We keep the research data until the capstone is completed and the resulting paper is published in a reputable research journal. After that, we keep it only for the period required by the University's records policy or the journal's data-availability policy, whichever is longer. Then we securely destroy it: printed forms are shredded; Google Forms responses are deleted; any research contact data in Supabase is deleted.

## 9. Data privacy

PakaonAI processes research data under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. The legal basis is your **consent**. The Privacy Policy explains your rights and how to exercise them: `https://pakaonai.syntaxure.dev/privacy` (draft pending University Legal Office review).

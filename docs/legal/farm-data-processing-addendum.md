## 2. Roles and scope

The Farm decides what farm data is collected, why it is processed, and who may access it. PakaonAI
processes the data only to provide the PakaonAI precision feeding service, secure it, and follow the
Farm’s documented instructions.

The data may include farm and pond records, biomass samples, feeding schedules and events, device
telemetry, harvest records, account details of Farm-authorized users, and audit logs.

## 3. Farm instructions

The Farm instructs PakaonAI to:

1. host, store, transmit, back up, and display the data;
2. calculate biomass, feed amounts, FCR, SGR, and related metrics;
3. send the feed configuration to the ESP32 feeder and receive telemetry;
4. provide support, security, and audit functions; and
5. aggregate or de-identify data only as permitted by the Terms and the Farm’s written consent.

PakaonAI will notify the Farm if an instruction appears to violate the Data Privacy Act or other law.

## 4. Confidentiality

PakaonAI will limit access to personnel and advisers who need it, bind them to confidentiality, and
not disclose the data to another party except as required by this Addendum, the Terms, or law.

## 5. Security

PakaonAI will maintain reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards, including
role-based access, least privilege, encrypted transport, hashed device tokens, rate limits, audit
logs, backups, and incident response. Printed research forms, if any, are kept in a locked cabinet
accessible only to the researchers.

## 6. Subprocessors

The Farm authorizes the processors listed in the Privacy Policy: Supabase (Singapore), Vercel
(Sydney), OpenCodeGo (AI inference; training opt-out enabled), Resend (email), and Google LLC
(Google Forms/Drive for research questionnaires, if used). PakaonAI will give the Farm reasonable
notice of a new processor that processes farm operational data.

## 7. Data-subject requests

The Farm is responsible for responding to requests from its workers and other data subjects.
PakaonAI will assist with access, correction, deletion, blocking, portability, or objection requests
within a reasonable time and will not respond directly to a data subject in a way that conflicts with
the Farm’s instructions, unless the law requires it.

## 8. Breach notification

PakaonAI will notify the Farm without undue delay, and in any event within the period required by
law, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Farm’s data. The notice will
describe the nature of the breach, the data involved, and the measures taken. The Farm is responsible
for notifying the NPC and affected data subjects where it is the PIC; PakaonAI will assist.

## 9. Return and deletion

On termination, PakaonAI will make the Farm’s data available for export for a reasonable period,
then delete or anonymize it according to the Terms and the Privacy Policy, unless a legal retention
obligation applies. Backup copies are deleted on the normal backup cycle.

## 10. Audit and records

PakaonAI will keep appropriate records of processing and security events and will provide the Farm
with reasonable information to demonstrate compliance, subject to confidentiality and security.

## 11. Liability and term

The liability, termination, and governing-law provisions of the Terms apply to this Addendum. This
Addendum takes effect on the date signed and continues while PakaonAI processes the Farm’s data.
